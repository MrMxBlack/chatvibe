# ChatVibe Analyzer

**Live site:** https://mrmxblack.github.io/chatvibe/

Turn a WhatsApp or Telegram chat export into a fun, private relationship report.

- Green Flag Score and Chat Chemistry
- Who texts more, who replies faster, who starts the chat
- Fun awards for every person, love / sorry / thanks / laughter meters
- Chat Wrapped slideshow, Versus Battle card, Guess Who Said It game
- Compare two chats side by side, Funniest Moments, Screenshot Story Studio
- Story-ready share cards (scores and counts only, never message text)
- 6 languages: English, Bangla, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, Bahasa Indonesia

## Privacy

Everything is calculated locally in your browser with JavaScript. Your chat file is never uploaded, there is no account, no tracking and no ads. You can verify this yourself: load the page, turn on airplane mode, and analyze a chat. It still works.

## How to use

1. Export a chat from WhatsApp (Without Media, .txt or .zip) or from Telegram Desktop (JSON format).
2. Drop the file on the site, or paste chat text, or try the built-in demo.
3. Read your report, play the game, download your cards, share the numbers.

## Notes

Single self-contained page (`index.html`). The only external requests are fonts and the JSZip library used to open .zip exports; chat data itself never leaves the device.
