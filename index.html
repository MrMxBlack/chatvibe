How every number is calculated

Messages: every export line that starts with a date, a time and a sender name counts as one message. Lines without a sender, such as group notices, do not count as messages. Words: runs of letters in the message text; media placeholder lines add no words. Reply time: when the sender changes, the gap to the previous message counts as that sender's reply, but only when the gap is under 12 hours, so an overnight restart does not destroy the picture. The report shows the median, the middle reply, which is steadier than an average. Conversation starts: the first message in the chat, and any message that arrives more than 6 hours after the one before it. Streak: the longest run of calendar days in a row that each have at least one message. Green Flag Score: a playful blend of balance between people, median reply speed, and warm signals such as questions, thanks, sorry and laughter. It is a fun summary of this export only, not a judgement of any person or relationship. Funniest Moments: messages earn points for laughter words, emojis, capital letter drama, exclamation marks, very long messages and being sent between midnight and 5 am. Large chats: there is no message cap in ChatVibe; very large exports may simply take longer on a slow phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is a WhatsApp chat analyzer private?

ChatVibe reads your export file inside your own browser with JavaScript. The file is never sent to a server, there is no account, and closing the tab clears the chat from memory. You can test this by loading the page, turning on airplane mode, and then analyzing a file.

How do I export a WhatsApp chat for analysis?

Open the chat in WhatsApp, tap the contact or group name or the three dot menu, choose More and then Export Chat, and pick Without Media. You get a .txt file on Android or a .zip on iPhone. Drop that file into ChatVibe.

Can I analyze a WhatsApp group chat?

Yes. ChatVibe shows up to 8 participants with per person message counts, replies, starts, emojis and fun awards. You can also tap a person to focus the whole report on them.

What does a WhatsApp chat analyzer show?

Message counts and share per person, words per message, median reply time, who starts conversations, top emojis, most used words, activity by hour, weekday and month, longest streak, longest silence, media, link and deleted message counts, plus fun scores like the Green Flag Score and Chat Chemistry.

Is ChatVibe free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no signup, no premium tier and no ads inside the tool.

Why does my export show zero messages?

Make sure you exported Without Media as a .txt or .zip file, not a backup. WhatsApp backups cannot be read by any analyzer. Also check that the file is a real chat export with dates, times and names at the start of each message line.

Can I analyze a Telegram chat?

Yes. In Telegram Desktop, open the chat menu and choose Export Chat History, pick the JSON format, then drop the result.json file into ChatVibe. The same report, scores, awards and Wrapped show are generated, still entirely on your device.

How can I see who texts first and who replies faster?

ChatVibe counts a conversation start whenever a message arrives more than 6 hours after the previous one, and credits it to that sender. Reply speed is the median time each person takes to answer when the sender changes, ignoring gaps over 12 hours. Both appear in the report for every participant.

Does ChatVibe work with iPhone WhatsApp exports?

Yes. iPhone exports arrive as a .zip file, and ChatVibe opens the zip in your browser and reads the chat inside it. Android .txt exports work directly too, in both cases with media excluded.
