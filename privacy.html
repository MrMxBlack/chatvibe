Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This policy covers the ChatVibe Analyzer website at mrmxblack.github.io/chatvibe.

The short version

ChatVibe analyzes your chat exports inside your own browser. Your chat files, messages, screenshots, and reports are never uploaded to a server, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone. There is no account, no sign up, and no tracking of what you analyze.

What we collect

Nothing. The analyzer runs entirely with JavaScript on your device. When you close or refresh the page, the chat data is gone from memory. We operate no database of chats, messages, or results, because the tool is built so that your data never reaches us in the first place.

What your browser stores locally

Only one small preference: the language you choose is saved in your browser local storage so the site opens in that language next time. No chat content is ever written to local storage, cookies, or any other storage by ChatVibe.

Screenshots and story cards

The Screenshot Story Studio opens your screenshot locally in your browser and draws your story card on your device. The screenshot is never uploaded. Share cards generated by ChatVibe contain only the scores, counts, names, and text that you choose to put on them.

Third party services used to load the page

Like almost every website, loading this page makes a small number of requests to content delivery networks: Google Fonts for the typefaces and cdnjs for the JSZip library that opens zip files. Those providers see the technical request (such as your IP address) the same way they would on any site that uses them. They receive none of your chat data, which never leaves your device.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web server information, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Advertising

ChatVibe currently shows no advertising inside the tool. If advertising is ever added to pages around the tool, this policy will be updated first, the analyzer itself will remain free of ads, and your chat data will still never be used for advertising, because it never leaves your device.

Children

ChatVibe collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Only analyze chats that you are part of, and share report numbers rather than private messages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page: github.com/MrMxBlack/chatvibe.
